Fine Line Projects is a Ballari-based group of architects, engineers, interior designers and construction consultants delivering building solutions across Karnataka.

Who We Are

A Group of Royal Constructions.

Fine Line Projects (also known as Fine Line Construction Projects) brings together architectural creativity, professional civil engineering, and disciplined construction execution. Every project we take on is treated as a long-term commitment to the client — not just a build.

From concept to completion, we provide end-to-end building solutions across residential, commercial, and interior design domains in Ballari and surrounding regions of Karnataka.
