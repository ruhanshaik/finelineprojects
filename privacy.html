Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Fine Line Projects (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use this website and contact us.

Information you provide

If you use our enquiry form, you may enter your name, phone number, email address, service interest, and project description. The form opens WhatsApp with a prepared message for you to review and send. This website does not submit that enquiry to or store it on its own server. Information you choose to send is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you submit an enquiry through our chatbot, the details you provide, your consent, and the page where you started are sent through our website hosting provider to our private Google spreadsheet so our team can respond. The chatbot does not ask for payment details or other sensitive personal information.

You may also contact us by phone or email. We use information you provide to respond to your enquiry and discuss our services.

Website preferences and service providers

The home page stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage. The site uses third-party services to load fonts, icons, animation libraries, images, and the map. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their content and handle it under their own privacy policies. Your browser or hosting provider may also process standard technical logs.

Cookies and your choices

This site does not use its own advertising or analytics cookies. You can clear local storage or block third-party content through your browser settings; some site features may then look or work differently.
